Case Studies

Selected Work

Replacing spreadsheets and paper with an operations platform

Sole developer and product owner for a construction contractor

What I built
  • An 8-module Flask and SQLAlchemy application covering payroll, billing, invoicing, estimating, scheduling, and more, with role-based access for Admin and Superintendent users.
  • The full revenue lifecycle, from payroll intake through billing items to invoices, with status-driven workflows, configurable tax and profit markup, and locked rate snapshots.
  • Automated generation of payroll exports, voucher workbooks, work orders, and change orders using openpyxl and python-docx.
  • An estimating engine rebuilt from the company's legacy bidding workbook and validated penny for penny against the original.
  • A self-migrating database schema and feature-flagged rollout of new modules.
The result

The platform replaced Excel and paper as the company's system of record and is used daily by every employee. Document prep that used to take hours each billing cycle is now generated automatically.

Stack: Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, SQLite, Jinja2, Bootstrap, pandas, openpyxl, python-docx

Catching supplier overbilling before it gets paid

Month-end invoice checks for the same construction contractor

The problem

Supplier invoices had to be checked by hand against purchase orders and agreed pricing, so overbilling, price drift, and tax mistakes were easy to miss at month-end.

What I built
  • A tool that matches each supplier invoice to its QuickBooks purchase order and the supplier's price sheet.
  • Automatic flags for overbilling, price variances, and tax errors, so review time goes to the exceptions.
