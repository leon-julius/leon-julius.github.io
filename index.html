Case Study

Replacing spreadsheets and paper with an operations platform

Sole developer and product owner for a construction contractor

What I built
  • A Flask and SQLAlchemy web application with 91 routes, 19 tables, and role-based access for Admin and Superintendent users.
  • The full revenue lifecycle, from payroll intake through billing items to invoices, with status-driven workflows, configurable tax and profit markup, and locked rate snapshots.
  • Automated generation of payroll exports, voucher workbooks, work orders, and change orders using openpyxl and python-docx.
  • An estimating engine rebuilt from the company's legacy bidding workbook and validated penny for penny against the original.
  • A self-migrating database schema and feature-flagged rollout of new modules.
The result

The platform is now the company's system of record for six core back-office workflows, and document prep that used to take hours each billing cycle is generated automatically.

Stack: Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, SQLite, Jinja2, Bootstrap, pandas, openpyxl, python-docx
